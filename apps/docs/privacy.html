Privacy

What www.ortho-earth.com collects, and why. Last updated: 2026-11-20.

Ortho Earth is an open-source project run by one person, Kenji Yoshida. You can use every map on this site without an account. The site does not show ads, does not sell data, and does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

1. Visit statistics

The site is served through Cloudflare, which inserts its Web Analytics script into pages. It counts page views, the page you came from, your country, and your browser and device type, without cookies. I use these totals to see which pages people read. Like any web host, Cloudflare also handles your IP address to deliver pages and to block attacks. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

2. Signing in (optional)

Some apps let you sign in with GitHub, Google or X to keep your own files and publish works. Only then does the site store:

  • your account ID at that service, your display name and profile picture, and your email address if the service provides it;
  • a sign-in session: a random ID in a cookie on your browser (kept for 30 days), and on the server only a hashed copy of it with your browser's user-agent string;
  • files you upload (up to 1 GB per person) and works you publish. Published works (title, link, author name, summary, thumbnail) are public by design.

This data is kept on Cloudflare (D1 database and R2 storage) and is used only to run these features. You can delete your files and works yourself at any time. To have your whole account removed, email kenji.yoshida.home.2026@gmail.com and say which service you signed in with.

3. Data kept only in your browser

To load faster, the maps keep downloaded map tiles in your browser's storage, and remember small settings such as whether your device can use WebGPU. This stays on your device and is never sent to the site. You can remove it by clearing this site's data in your browser.

4. Map data from other services

Some maps load data directly from public data providers (for example map tiles, satellite and earthquake feeds, PLATEAU 3D city models or GSI data). Your browser then connects to those services, which see your IP address under their own policies.

5. The MCP server for AI agents

The server at www.ortho-earth.com/mcp only answers read-only questions about Ortho Earth. It does not store what you ask.

6. Changes and contact

If this changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change. Contact: kenji.yoshida.home.2026@gmail.com.
