Sandcastle v2.2.0

A comparison of the official CesiumJS Sandcastle examples run on Ortho Earth — not the official Sandcastle.

Each of the 302 examples runs unchanged: once on CesiumJS 1.146.0, once on the Ortho Earth globe through its Cesium-compatible facade (@ortho-earth/cesium: Cesium's own coordinates, time, CZML, entities and 3D Tiles loading, with Ortho Earth doing the drawing), at the same time and from the same camera. Each card shows CesiumJS on the left and Ortho Earth on the right. Click a card for the larger pictures and the example on the official Sandcastle.
